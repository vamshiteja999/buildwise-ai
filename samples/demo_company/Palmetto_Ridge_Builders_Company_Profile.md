# Palmetto Ridge Builders, LLC (fictional demo company)

Note: This is a fictional company created for software demonstrations. It does not describe a real business.

## About
Palmetto Ridge Builders is a general contractor based in Boca Raton, Florida, founded in 2009. It builds light commercial buildings, medical offices and multifamily projects in Palm Beach and Broward counties. The company employs 42 people: 6 project managers, 9 superintendents, 3 safety staff and 24 office and field staff.

## Licenses and insurance
- Florida Certified General Contractor license number CGC-DEMO-0000 (fictional), held by the qualifying agent Dana Whitfield.
- General liability insurance: $2,000,000 per occurrence, $4,000,000 aggregate, policy renews on March 1 each year.
- Workers' compensation: statutory limits. The company's experience modification rate (EMR) is 0.82.
- Umbrella liability: $5,000,000.
- Bonding capacity: $12,000,000 single project, $30,000,000 aggregate.

## Services
Preconstruction and estimating, construction management at risk, design-build, tenant improvements, and 12-month warranty service.

## Safety program
The company follows OSHA 29 CFR 1926. All field staff complete the OSHA 10-hour course, and superintendents complete the 30-hour course. Fall protection is required at 6 feet or more above a lower level. The company recorded 0 lost-time injuries in 2025 and a total recordable incident rate (TRIR) of 1.1.

## Payment terms
Subcontractors are paid within 30 days of an approved pay application. Retainage is 10 percent until 50 percent completion and 5 percent after that. Lien waivers are required with every pay application.
