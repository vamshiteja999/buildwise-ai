PALMETTO RIDGE BUILDERS - WEEKLY TOOLBOX TALK (fictional demo document)
Topic: Fall protection on roofs and open edges

1. Scope
This talk applies to every worker exposed to a fall of 6 feet or more to a lower level, including roof edges, floor openings and leading edges.

2. Required protection
Before work starts, the superintendent must choose one of these: a guardrail system, a safety net system, or a personal fall arrest system. Warning lines alone are allowed only for roofing work on low-slope roofs, set back at least 6 feet from the edge.

3. Daily inspection
A competent person inspects anchors, lanyards and harnesses before every shift. Any harness that has arrested a fall is removed from service immediately and tagged.

4. Rescue
A written rescue plan is required. The plan names who will perform a rescue and how a suspended worker is reached within 15 minutes.

5. Weather
Roof work stops when sustained winds exceed 25 mph or when lightning is within 10 miles.

6. Reporting
Report every near miss to the safety director, Marcus Lee, on the same day.
Sign-in: all attendees must sign the sheet kept in the project trailer.
